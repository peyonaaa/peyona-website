About Fiona

My goal is to become a web developer who turns ideas into innovative and meaningful digital experiences.

VendoMachine

A machine that exchanges recyclable waste for coins. Our Senior High capstone, March 2025.

Problem

Improper waste disposal and low interest in recycling add to pollution. We wanted a reason for people to recycle.

Solution

A waste-to-coin exchange machine that accepts recyclables and pays out coins, promoting recycling and responsible waste management through technology.

What I learned

Research, teamwork, creativity and problem-solving, and that technology can solve real problems close to home.
